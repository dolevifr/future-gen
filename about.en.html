About us

Our vision, and the people behind FutureGen Academy

🚀

Our vision

Today's tech world is moving at a dizzying pace, and it calls for a toolkit completely different from the one traditional education offers. At FutureGen Academy, our vision is to take the next generation and prepare it for the real world. We believe that in an age when artificial intelligence makes a "one-person company" possible, every child deserves the chance to discover and develop their own unique strengths. That's why we created an elite programme designed to build entrepreneurial thinking, give them self-confidence and leadership, and teach them financial literacy from a young age – practical tools that will stay with them and give them a real edge in whatever path they choose.

🤝

Who we are

Behind the venture are Yariv Frajun and Dolev Ifrah, entrepreneurs with rich, day-to-day experience in high-tech and technology. Working in the industry, we saw the huge gap between today's theoretical tools and the skills it really takes to succeed out there. We founded the programme out of a genuine passion for bringing the knowledge, experience and working methods of the high-tech world straight to kids. We're here to pass on the right mindset, teach them the language of business, and give them the personal guidance that will turn them from passive consumers of technology into creators and leaders.
