Accessibility statement

for Future-gen.co.il

Making the site accessible

The Future-gen.co.il team works to meet the accessibility guidelines of Israeli Standard IS 5568, which follows WCAG 2.0 AA.

The accessibility menu

You can open the accessibility menu with the accessibility icon shown on the site, or with the Tab key. The menu includes:

  • Languages – choose the menu's display language
  • Accessibility profiles – ready-made sets of adjustments
  • Main options – display and content adjustments
  • Feedback form – report problems or make requests
  • Accessibility statement – view this document

The accessibility menu was developed and tested by accessibility experts, and is built to support a range of assistive technologies.

Accessibility services and features on the site

The site includes a wide range of accessibility adjustments:

  • Full keyboard navigation
  • Alternative text (alt text) for all images
  • Proper colour contrast
  • Accessible semantic HTML
  • Stopping animations
  • Reducing dynamic effects
  • Several contrast modes
  • Saturation control (high, low or monochrome)
  • Readable fonts
  • Text size and spacing adjustments
  • Text alignment by preference
  • Highlighted links and headings
  • A large mouse cursor
  • A reading mask and reading guide
  • Skip links and ARIA structures
  • Screen reader adjustments
  • Accessible forms with clear labels
  • Help descriptions for interactive elements
  • An option to hide images
  • Menu language support
  • An accessible feedback form

Updates, testing and adjustments

The site's accessibility system runs continuously and applies accessibility adjustments to new components and to components that have changed.

The site has undergone a comprehensive accessibility review and manual adjustments by Tabnav's accessibility experts. Periodic manual accessibility checks are also carried out, and further adjustments are made based on their findings, to improve accessibility and keep the experience as accessible as possible.

For more information about the site's accessibility, or to receive the digital accessibility certificate, contact the accessibility coordinator using the details at the end of this statement.

On-site accessibility arrangements

The company does not receive visitors at its offices; the service is provided online and/or by phone.
