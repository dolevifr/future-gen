🧠 What they learn

The programme's three threads

Every session touches at least one of them, and the final projects tie all three together.

Swipe sideways

💫

Generative AI

Technology in practice — we do not just play with AI, we learn to use it like professionals to create content, build brands and save time

What it covers…

  • Prompt engineering: how to "talk" to the machine, write precise instructions and get top-tier results
  • A broad digital toolbox: hands-on use of advanced AI tools for text, images, sound and video, and matching the right tool to the need
  • Building a brand from scratch: a complete brand identity — name, logo, colours and business slogan
  • Storytelling to video: techniques for animating images into professional video that holds an audience
  • Real business application: fast AI-driven market research and building clean, focused investor decks
🎤

Leadership and life skills

The toolkit for success — technology is only the instrument. To really succeed we develop the highest level of personal skills

What it covers…

  • Presenting: confidence and the ability to deliver a message to a class or an audience clearly and without fear
  • The art of persuasion: how to speak compellingly, tell the story right and win the trust of "investors"
  • Body language and voice: the techniques the world's best speakers use — posture, eye contact, tone and gesture
  • Building self-belief: a boutique group of up to 15 creates a safe space to leave the comfort zone, dare, fail and grow
📈

Entrepreneurship, product and finance

Think like a CEO — the mindset of the world's leading startup founders, alongside a real understanding of the language of money

What it covers…

  • From a problem to a profitable business: spotting real-world problems, defining them and turning them into a winning idea
  • Market research and product definition: what customers actually need, what competitors do, and how to design a product that solves the pain
  • Working the way tech companies do: an introduction to Agile — the methods, the flexibility and the fast thinking of technology firms
  • Inspiration from business leaders: learning from the paths, mistakes and successes of history's great founders
  • Financial literacy for ages 13–16: an informed introduction to investing, the stock market, property fundamentals, and the power of compound interest

❓ Questions & answers

Frequently asked questions

What's the difference between the 10–12 and 13–16 groups?
Each group has its own syllabus, built for its age. For ages 10–12, semester 1 is about creating with AI — comics, branding, presentations and film — up to the Oscars night, and semester 2 goes from building a first game to founding a startup, up to the "Shark Tank" finale. For ages 13–16, semester 1 is a branding and content studio built on AI, and semester 2 is an entrepreneurship and finance lab: product management, a landing page, a business model, tax, the stock market, property and personal money, up to demo day. Both groups have 28 sessions and two showcase events, and each group's full syllabus is on the syllabus page.
Is prior coding or design experience needed?
No. The programme starts from absolute zero — the first session is the first time most students write a professional prompt, and semester 2 is the first time they build a real venture. Everything builds up step by step.
What equipment is needed at home?
A laptop or desktop with an internet connection, headphones and a microphone. A webcam is nice but not required. Some tools need an up-to-date browser — that's it.
How many students per group?
Up to 15 children. Learning happens in small boutique groups, so every child gets close mentoring and every voice is heard.
How long is each session?
Each session lasts 60 minutes. Sessions run once a week, live and online, with 28 sessions across the school year.
Can we cancel mid-year?
It depends on how you pay. On the monthly option (₪350) you can cancel at any time with no penalty: the cancellation takes effect within 3 business days, and you pay only up to then. The annual subscription (₪300 a month) carries a commitment to the school year in exchange for the lower price; leaving early costs a cancellation fee of 5% of the remaining balance or ₪100, whichever is lower.
Is there a discount for siblings or family members?
Of course! We offer a discount for family members. For all the details, reach out on WhatsApp, by phone or by email and we'll be happy to help.
Who are the instructors?
Professional instructors from the tech industry with deep technology experience, who pass practical knowledge to the kids straight from the field.
